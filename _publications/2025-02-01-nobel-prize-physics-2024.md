The 2024 Nobel Prize in Physics honored John Hopfield and Geoffrey Hinton for foundational discoveries that enabled today's deep learning revolution. Written for a broad physics audience, this popular science article traces the deep intellectual connections between physics and neural networks — from statistical mechanics inspiration to modern applications in first-principles materials simulations — and offers a glimpse into how deep learning is reshaping the way physicists understand and predict the quantum world.
